Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 11/26/2024
Last Updated: 10/21/2026

Big Time Pixels LLC owns and operates various mobile applications, including Rooms AI Compatibility, Color Contacts, and other applications published under Big Time Pixels LLC. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you use our applications.

Information We Collect

Depending on the application and features you use, we may collect the following information:

  • Account information, such as email address and profile details
  • AI Interview responses, where applicable
  • User-provided content such as responses, messages, and photos
  • App usage and technical data, such as app version, activity/usage and crash logs

Not all applications collect or require the same information. Information is collected only as necessary to provide the features and functionality of the specific application.

Our applications do not track users across apps or websites.

Face Data and TrueDepth API Usage

The Rooms AI Compatibility application uses live-captured face data obtained through Apple's TrueDepth API during the identity verification process.

Face data is used only to confirm that a real person is present while a verification photo is being taken. This data is processed in real time on the device and is not stored, retained, or transmitted.

The verification photo submitted by the user is stored only for the duration necessary for manual review by our verification team. Once verification is approved or rejected, the photo is permanently deleted.

Face data is never shared with third parties and is not used for advertising, tracking, analytics, or any purpose beyond identity verification.

This section applies only to applications that utilize Apple's TrueDepth API for identity verification.

How We Use Information

Information is used only to:

  • Provide and operate our applications
  • Generate Rooms and compatibility insights, where applicable
  • Perform verification, where applicable
  • Provide requested application features and functionality
  • Improve app functionality and reliability

Personal data is not used for advertising purposes.

Contact Data and Privacy

Certain applications, including Color Contacts, may request access to your device's contacts to provide features that allow you to personalize or update contact information and photos.

We understand that your contacts contain personal and private information. Protecting this information is important to us.

When an application requests access to your contacts:

  • Local Processing: Contact information is accessed and processed locally on your device solely to provide the requested application functionality.
  • No Data Collection: Big Time Pixels LLC does not collect or transmit your contact names, phone numbers, email addresses, photos, or other contact details to our servers or external services.
  • No Data Storage: Contact information is not stored, retained, or copied to any database or server operated by Big Time Pixels LLC. Any changes made to your contacts are saved directly to your device's contact database.
  • No Data Sharing: Contact information is never sold, shared with, or disclosed to third parties by Big Time Pixels LLC.
  • No Secondary Use: Contact information is never used for advertising, analytics, tracking, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the application's contact-related functionality.
  • User Control: Access to contacts requires your permission through your device's operating system. You may revoke or modify this permission at any time through your device's Settings.

Our applications only access contact information as necessary to perform the actions you request. Big Time Pixels LLC does not maintain external copies of your contacts.

Your contact information remains on your device and is never transmitted to Big Time Pixels LLC.

Data Sharing

Big Time Pixels LLC does not sell personal data.

Information may be shared strictly as necessary if required by law.

Contact information accessed through device permissions is not collected or transmitted to Big Time Pixels LLC and is not shared with third parties.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only as long as necessary to provide the service. User data is not retained after account deletion.

For applications that access device contacts, contact information is processed locally and is not retained by Big Time Pixels LLC. Revoking contact permissions prevents further access by the application unless permission is granted again.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated. Continued use of our applications indicates acceptance of the updated policy.
